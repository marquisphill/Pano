Small pots from a small studio

Mugs, bowls and planters, thrown by hand in a made-up village. This is an example site for the Paño kit: the studio does not exist and nothing here is for sale.

See our classes

What we make

Mugs

Speckled glaze, a handle that fits two fingers.

Bowls

Wide and shallow, for soup or for fruit.

Planters

With a hole in the bottom, as plants prefer.

About the studio

Two wheels, one kiln and a lot of tea. Everything is made, glazed and fired in the room behind the shop.
